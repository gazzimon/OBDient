# OBDient — We're building the brain of the car 🚗🧠

> 📄 [README](./README.md) · 🎤 [Pitch](./PITCH.md) · **🧠 Vision (you are here)**

> Every car already has a nervous system. We're giving it a mind.

Modern cars are full of sensors. They feel everything — temperature, pressure,
misfires, vibration — and say almost nothing. When something goes wrong, you get a
cryptic orange light and a trip to a shop that charges you to read a code your car
already knew.

**OBDient turns that silent nervous system into intelligence.** Today it plugs into
your car, listens in real time, and explains what's happening in plain language —
with an AI that runs **entirely on your phone**, privately, even with no signal.

That's the product you can use today. But the diagnosis is just the first neuron.
The real project is bigger:

## The one-line vision

**We're building the brain of the car — a private, on-device intelligence that
understands the vehicle, perceives the world around it, and eventually thinks
together with every other car on the road.**

## Where we are today ✅

A working brain stem — the parts that already run on real hardware, in a real car:

- **It listens.** Reads 20 live engine signals through a standard OBD-II adapter.
- **It understands.** A self-trained AI model ("CARpsy") explains faults like a
  mechanic would — on your phone, offline, no cloud required.
- **It learns.** Every answer you rate 👍/👎 makes the car's knowledge sharper and
  more trustworthy over time — without ever sending your data away.
- **It's private by default.** Your car's data never leaves the device without your
  explicit permission.

This is the proof that an intelligent car doesn't need the cloud, a subscription, or
your privacy. It can all live in your pocket.

## Phase 1 — Training in the workshop 🔧
### Audience: mechanics & repair shops

This is the product **today**, deployed where the best automotive knowledge already
lives: the workshop. A mechanic plugs OBDient into a car, gets an instant AI
diagnosis, and — crucially — **confirms or corrects it** with professional judgment.

Mechanics are the perfect teachers: high volume, real cars, expert verdicts. Each
confirmed diagnosis becomes ground-truth that feeds the brain.

### The virtuous cycle ♻️

```

        │                                                 │

   Claude teaches  ──►  Human / car verifies  ──►  SHIMI accumulates
```

> **Claude teaches → human/car verifies → SHIMI accumulates → CARpsy is retrained

Every turn of this loop makes CARpsy a little more self-sufficient and the cloud a
little less necessary. The brain is literally getting cheaper and smarter at the
same time. **This is the data engine that powers everything after it.**

## Phase 2 — CARpsy graduates: the brain of the car 🧠
### Audience: every driver

Once the loop has run enough times, CARpsy is **good enough to stand on its own** —
no cloud, no workshop, no subscription. That's when it stops being a mechanic's tool
and becomes a **consumer product: the brain and the voice of the car itself.**

This is the maximum objective — **the Alexa of the car**, but private and on-device:

> *"Hey — your coolant is creeping up, there's a slow leak. You've got maybe 40 km
> before it matters. Want me to find a shop on the way?"*

You talk to your car. Your car answers — in plain language, out loud, hands-free,
and it actually **understands what's wrong with itself.** From here, the brain keeps
growing:

### It moves into the car 🍓
The brain no longer needs your phone. A fully-trained CARpsy runs on a **computer the
size of a credit card** — a Raspberry Pi wired into the vehicle. The car is now
permanently intelligent: always listening, always learning, always yours.

### It grows senses 📡
The brain fuses engine data with the sensors around it — **GPS, accelerometer,
motion and impact sensors**. Now it knows not just *how the engine feels*, but **how
the car is moving, where it is, and what just happened to it.**

### It can save your life 🚨
If the brain senses a crash — a sudden impact, a stopped car, no response — it can
**automatically call emergency services, share your exact location, and report what
happened**, even if you can't. The voice that diagnoses you becomes the voice that
protects you.

### The brains start talking to each other 🕸️
Each car carries its own intelligence — so cars can **talk directly to one another**,
no central server, no company watching. The car ahead hits black ice and the cars
behind it *already know*. A fault diagnosed on one car spreads the fix to **every
other car like it.** A **mesh of cars, each with its own private AI, sharing
knowledge instead of data** — a collective intelligence owned by drivers, not
corporations.

## Phase 3 — The self-healing network 🔧🤝
### Audience: drivers helping drivers

A brain that knows what's wrong is good. A network that **fixes it** is the goal.

Once cars share intelligence, the next step is sharing *help*. Your car detects that
the one beside you is about to be stranded — a flat tire, a dying battery, an engine
minutes away from burning out — and the network does what no tow truck can: it
**dispatches someone who already knows exactly what's broken.**

From a neighbor with a jack to a certified mechanic, summoned by the network itself —
an **"Uber of technicians"** where the diagnosis arrives *before* the help does.

The moat is the diagnosis. Roadside services arrive blind and figure it out on site.
OBDient's helper shows up **pre-briefed**: the fault code, the symptom, the likely
part. And the killer move is **prevention** — the brain predicts the catastrophic
failure and sends help *before* it happens. This isn't roadside assistance. It's an
**immune system for the road.** Cars don't just talk to each other — **they take care
of each other.**

## The economic layer — agents that pay each other 💸
### Powered by x402 on Tether's stablecoin

A decentralized brain needs a decentralized economy. Today, contributing knowledge to
the shared network is pure altruism. **x402 turns it into a living market.**

x402 is an open standard for **machine-to-machine micropayments in stablecoin** —
built exactly for AI agents that pay one another for a service. In OBDient, when one
car's agent consumes verified diagnostic knowledge from another's, it **pays a tiny
micropayment for the answer.** Contribute knowledge that helps other cars, and you
**earn.**

This is a natural fit for the **Tether ecosystem**: on-device QVAC agents settling in
**USD₮**, no middleman, no central server.

It also makes the network *self-correcting*:

- **Quality gets priced in.** You pay more for higher-confidence, peer-verified
  knowledge — so the trust-gating, quorum, and confidence scores that already exist
  become an economic signal, not just a technical one.
- **Spam stops paying.** Junk knowledge never reaches quorum, so it never earns.
- **Privacy is preserved.** Settlement is pseudonymous and aggregated — payments
  never re-attach a VIN or identity to the anonymous knowledge chunks.

Drivers and workshops don't just *use* the network — they **own a stake in it.**
